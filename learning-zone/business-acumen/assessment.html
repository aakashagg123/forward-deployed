    How scoring works

    Each axis has four statements. Its score is the average of your answered ratings, mapped from 1–5 to 0–100%: (average − 1) ÷ 4 × 100. With all four answered, that equals (total − 4) ÷ 16 × 100. The plan ranks axes by gap (100% minus the score). Below 50% is a focus area, with a lesson link and one two-week action from that lesson's practitioner checklist. From 50% to 74% is a build area. At 75% or above, the plan suggests how to maintain or stretch. The method is explained in lesson 1.
