# About me

I'm Aakash — a product leader and builder based in Mumbai, working at the intersection of fintech, AI, and systems that have to hold up under real-world pressure.

I currently lead the lending origination platform, CRM, and AI charter at JSW One Finance, a digital-first NBFC serving India's MSME ecosystem. Commercial lending here is 100% digital, lead to disbursement, and I'm one of the earliest product hires building that out:

- Channel finance, vendor finance, and term loan origination
- The lending API suite
- The customer master that ties it all together

## What I actually do

My work has a familiar rhythm, whatever the company:

- Take an ambiguous problem and cut through the noise
- Design the system around it
- Drive adoption until it becomes second nature

It's not just about taming chaos — it's about giving teams the confidence and clarity to scale without losing their edge.

Across fintech, e-commerce, and insurance, I've shipped 0→1 products, led cross-functional teams, shaped complex integrations, and built workflows that survive contact with the real world:

- **JSW One MSME's B2B marketplace** — designed the core order-creation journey, with deep integrations across Salesforce, ERP, discounts, and pricing, supporting the business through its run to a $1B exit rate
- **Marsh** — led BenefitsCircle and QoverPro, shipping embedded insurance programs for names like Amazon, Ashok Leyland, and Cleartrip
- **AutoO2** — co-founded a B2B claims SaaS platform for motor insurers, built and grew it to 500+ onboarded workshops, and won an equity-free grant from the Government of Maharashtra's Mumbai Fintech program

## What I'm doing right now

AI has become a core part of how I operate — not as a shortcut, but as mental leverage. It helps me think wider, test assumptions faster, and push further than I could working alone. At JSW One Finance that shows up directly: AI is one of the product groups I lead, and it's genuinely at the core of some industry-first lending products we're building right now.

I also wrote that idea into a book: *[Prompt engineering guide for product managers](https://amzn.in/d/04Wv46eH)* — a practical guidebook for AI-driven productivity, aimed at senior PMs and product leaders learning to work with tools like ChatGPT as more than a brainstorming shortcut. Published May 2025, available on Kindle.
