# Prompt engineering guide for product managers

*A practical guidebook for AI-driven productivity.*

Welcome to prompt engineering guidebook for product managers (and product leadership alike), an instructional and conversational guide designed for senior product managers & product leaders. The rise of AI tools like ChatGPT has introduced powerful ways to streamline our work – from brainstorming ideas to drafting product documents in minutes. But leveraging these tools effectively requires a new skill: prompt engineering. In simple terms, prompt engineering is *the art and science of crafting effective inputs or "prompts" to guide AI models toward useful outputs.* Just as asking the right questions in a user interview yields better insights, phrasing your queries properly to an AI can be the difference between a game-changing suggestion and a generic response.

In this eBook, we'll build a strong foundation in prompt engineering and then dive into strategic applications tailored to product management. You'll find real-world use cases, prompt templates, and even printable worksheets to incorporate AI into your daily workflow. We'll also uncover some lesser-known (but high-impact) tips, show examples of effective vs. ineffective prompts, and highlight common mistakes to avoid. By the end, you'll understand how prompt engineering can maximize your productivity and professional leverage – enabling you as a product leader to spend less time on grunt work and more on high-level strategy.

Whether you're using ChatGPT or any other AI assistant, the principles in this guide apply broadly. Let's embark on this journey to turn AI into your trusty co-pilot in the product management process.

> Note: This guide uses examples with ChatGPT for clarity, but the techniques are applicable to similar AI tools. Citations are provided throughout to ground advice in established best practices and real examples.
